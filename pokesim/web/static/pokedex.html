About these counts

Loading catch records…

DV ratings use the sum of HP, Attack, Defense, Speed, and Special out of 75. HP is derived from the other four DVs. 1★: 0–37, 2★: 38–59, 3★: 60–74, 4★: 75 (perfect). Ratings measure fixed potential, independent of level and training.

Unknown DVs receive no rating. Browse your 3★ and 4★ partners ↗

Reaching level 100 also marks that Pokémon’s earlier forms. Each branch has its own finish.

Every DV at 15. Rare partners worth keeping.

Perfect finds are protected from automatic release and trading.

Level 100 milestones, 3★+ species, and perfect species stay recorded after evolution, trading, restarting the app, or loading an older save. Starting a fresh adventure resets them. DV species records credit only the species actually observed. Past released Pokémon with no record cannot be reconstructed.

The perfect-find count is a confirmed minimum. It includes existing partners and verified new catches. Old released Pokémon cannot be reconstructed, and identical gifts or trades after a partner leaves may be indistinguishable. Replayed catches do not count twice.
